# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- **Clear & Concise**: Keep copy direct, simple, and action-oriented. Avoid technical jargon.
- **Tone**: Helpful, reliable, and straightforward.

## Branding
- **Primary Colors**: Green (representing the G-Train) and dark grey/black.
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif fonts (e.g., Roboto or similar) that are highly readable on mobile screens.
- **Visual Identity**: Modern and minimalistic, focusing heavily on readability and quick information retrieval.

## UX Principles
- **Glanceability**: Users should understand train status and arrivals within 3 seconds of opening the app.
- **Consistency**: Use standard Material 3 components and patterns provided by Jetpack Compose.
- **Error Handling**: Fail gracefully during network issues and clearly communicate connection status or data staleness.
- **Accessibility**: High contrast for readability in sunlight, adequate touch target sizes (minimum 48dp), and support for screen readers.
